## 1. Executive Summary & One-Liner
**SolWork is an autonomous AI data microtasking and escrow protocol on Solana that enables African developers and students to earn in sub-second SPL USDC, verified by an on-chain Multimodal AI Oracle, with instant automated withdrawals to Nigerian bank accounts (OPay, Kuda) in under 30 seconds.**

---

## 2. Problem Statement
In Nigeria, hundreds of thousands of English-fluent, STEM-educated university students and graduates face extreme barriers to global digital labor:
1. **High Take-Rates & Exploitation**: Centralized platforms (Upwork, Remotasks, Scale AI, Appen) extract 15% to 25% in platform take-rates.
2. **Settlement Delays**: Payouts take 7 to 14 days to clear international banking rails and local FX wire transfers.
3. **Arbitrary Account Freezes**: Nigerian freelancers are routinely banned or had funds withheld by PayPal, Upwork, and Payoneer without recourse.
4. **The Web3 Usability Barrier**: Most Nigerian students are intimidated by crypto seed phrases, gas fees, and the risk of P2P fraud on centralized exchanges.

---

## 3. The SolWork v2 Solution
SolWork abstracts away all crypto friction while maintaining 100% non-custodial decentralized settlement on the Solana Virtual Machine (SVM):

* **"Invisible Web3" Onboarding**: Users sign in with Google in 1 tap via Turnkey MPC embedded keypairs. Zero seed phrase anxiety.
* **Autonomous Multimodal AI Evaluation Oracle**: When a worker submits a task (African dialect audio transcription, Python/Rust code RLHF, computer vision labeling), our Multimodal LLM-as-a-Judge audits the work in 3 seconds against the client's rubric & negative constraints. It issues an Ed25519 cryptographic attestation hash that unlocks the Anchor PDA escrow on-chain.
* **Instant Nigerian Fiat Off-Ramping**: Workers can click "Cash Out" and withdraw directly in Nigerian Naira to OPay, Kuda, PalmPay, or GTBank in 20 seconds at fair market rates (₦1,685/USDC) via Kotani Pay & the MultiHopper B2B router.
* **Ultra-Low Cost & Sub-Second Settlement**: Solana's 400ms slot times allow micro-payouts ($0.10 to $5.00) with sub-penny transaction fees that are impossible on Ethereum or Layer 2s.
